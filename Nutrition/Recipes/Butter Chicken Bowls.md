# Butter Chicken Bowls

Jar sauce shortcut — Aldi butter chicken / curry jar + chicken + Instant Pot rice.

## Ingredients
- 1.5–2 lb chicken thighs or breasts, cubed
- 1 jar butter chicken or curry sauce
- 1 cup dry white rice
- 0.5 cup Greek yogurt (optional, stir in at end or as topping)
- 1 cup frozen vegetables (optional)
- Oil for the pan
- Salt to taste

## Method
1. Start Instant Pot rice.
2. In a frying pan, cook chicken in a little oil until mostly done.
3. Pour in jar sauce; simmer 5–10 minutes until chicken is cooked through and sauce is hot.
4. Optional: stir in a spoon of Greek yogurt off heat for creaminess, or serve on top.
5. Serve over rice with heated frozen veg on the side.

## Notes
- Chickpeas optional add-in if you bought them.
- Leftovers reheat well for lunch next day.
