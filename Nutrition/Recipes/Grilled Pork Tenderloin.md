# Grilled Pork Tenderloin

Uses the marinated pork tenderloins already on hand. Propane grill. Pair with frozen veg + Instant Pot rice.

## Ingredients
- 1–2 marinated pork tenderloins (on hand)
- 2 cups cooked white rice (Instant Pot)
- 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables or broccoli
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: salsa or leftover chili on the side

## Method
1. Preheat propane grill to medium-high.
2. Grill tenderloins, turning occasionally, until internal temp hits 145°F (about 15–20 min depending on thickness).
3. Rest 5 minutes; slice.
4. Meanwhile: Instant Pot rice (usual white rice setting) and heat frozen veg (microwave, pan, or air fryer).
5. Plate pork with rice and veg.

## Notes
- Deferred Sat 9/5 (too late after Houndstown); aim for Sun or midweek when there’s daylight.
- Leftover slices → burrito bowls or salad next day.
- Second tenderloin: grill same night or Thu.
