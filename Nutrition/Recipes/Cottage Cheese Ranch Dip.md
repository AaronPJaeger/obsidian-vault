# Cottage Cheese Ranch Dip

Dip or bowl sauce — veggies, wraps, burrito bowls.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1–2 tbsp salsa or a squeeze of lemon
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 0.5 tsp onion powder
- 0.5 tsp dried dill or Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper
- Optional: splash of hot sauce

## Method
1. Blend everything until mostly smooth (leave a little texture if you like).
2. Taste and salt. Chill 10 min if you can — flavors wake up.
3. Use as: veggie dip, wrap smear, burrito-bowl drizzle, or chili side instead of sour cream.

## Notes
- Pair with bag salad / frozen veg / pork leftovers.
- High protein, stays in the deficit lane.
