# Instant Pot Chili

Batch chili — Aaron’s usual. Canned beans 2:1 kidney to pinto.

## Ingredients
- 1–1.5 lb ground beef
- 2 cans red kidney beans, drained
- 1 can pinto beans, drained
- 1–2 cans diced tomatoes (with juice)
- 1 onion, diced (optional)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper
- 0.5 cup water or broth if needed

## Method
1. On sauté, brown the ground beef; drain excess fat if needed.
2. Stir in onion and garlic until soft (skip if going dump-and-go).
3. Add beans, tomatoes, spices, and a splash of water/broth. Stir.
4. Pressure cook on high 15 minutes; natural release 10 minutes, then quick release.
5. Taste and adjust salt/spice. Serve with **~½ cup cottage cheese mixed on top** (default — sour cream swap, protein boost).

## Notes
- Cottage cheese finish is Aaron’s preferred serve — mixes in well, makes it a protein powerhouse.
- Leftovers: fridge 3–4 days; freeze portions.
- Chili pizza: spoon onto cheese pizza, add cheese, air-fry/bake until hot.
- Kitchen: Instant Pot; ≤20 min hands-on.
