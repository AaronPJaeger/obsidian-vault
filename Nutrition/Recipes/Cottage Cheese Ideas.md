# Cottage Cheese Ideas

High-protein add-ons for Aaron’s meals (not just a plain bowl).

## Fast mixes
- Chili topper — dollop instead of sour cream
- Burrito bowls — mix with salsa, spoon over rice/beans
- Fruit + cinnamon + scoop protein (dessert bowl)
- Scramble: fold into eggs at the end (makes them creamy)

## Blended sauces (immersion blender / small food processor)
See [[Cottage Cheese Alfredo]] and [[Cottage Cheese Ranch Dip]].
