# Chicken Burrito Bowls

Fast high-protein bowls. Instant Pot rice/beans + pan or air-fryer chicken.

## Ingredients
- 1.5–2 lb chicken thighs or breasts
- 1 cup dry white rice (or cooked rice on hand)
- 1 cup dried black beans (soaked/cooked) or 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1 cup Greek yogurt or cottage cheese (optional topping)
- 1 bag salad or shredded lettuce (optional)
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 0.5 tsp salt
- Oil for the pan

## Method
1. Cook rice in Instant Pot (usual white rice). Cook beans if using dried (IP beans setting) or warm canned beans.
2. Season chicken with chili powder, cumin, salt. Cook in frying pan or air fryer until done (~165°F); slice or shred.
3. Build bowls: rice, beans, chicken, salsa, cheese.
4. Add yogurt/cottage cheese and bag salad if you want.

## Notes
- Make extra rice/chicken for leftovers Wed/Fri.
- Hands-on ~20–25 min if rice/beans are already going.
