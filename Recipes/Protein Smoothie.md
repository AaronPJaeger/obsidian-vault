# Protein Smoothie

Quick breakfast with powder, creatine, yogurt, and fruit.

## Ingredients
- 1–1.5 scoops protein powder
- 1 serving creatine (as you usually take it)
- 0.5–1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 cup frozen fruit (berries or mix)
- 0.5–1 banana or handful fresh fruit (optional)
- 0.75–1.25 cups water or milk to blend
- Ice if using mostly fresh fruit

## Method
1. Add liquid first, then yogurt, fruit, powder, creatine.
2. Blend until smooth. Adjust liquid for thickness.
3. Drink now or pour into a bottle for the car.

## Notes
- No hard-boiled eggs as a side preference — smoothie stands alone.
- Cottage cheese can swap part of the yogurt if you want it thicker.
