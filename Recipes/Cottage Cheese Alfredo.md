# Cottage Cheese Alfredo

Creamy pasta/rice/chicken sauce — high protein, low effort.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 0.25 cup milk or pasta water
- 1 clove garlic (or 0.5 tsp garlic powder)
- 0.25 cup grated Parmesan or shredded cheese
- 0.5 tsp salt
- Black pepper
- Optional: pinch Italian seasoning

## Method
1. Blend cottage cheese, milk, garlic, salt, and pepper until smooth (immersion blender or small food processor).
2. Warm in a pan on low; stir in Parmesan until melted. Don’t boil hard or it can get grainy.
3. Toss with chicken, butter chicken leftovers over less sauce, or zucchini/pasta. Thin with a splash of milk if needed.

## Notes
- ~25–30 g protein per cup of cottage cheese before add-ins.
- Great on leftover chicken or as a white sauce over rice instead of heavy cream.
- Kitchen: immersion blender / small food processor; ≤10 min.
