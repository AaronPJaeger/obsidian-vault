# Recipes

Aaron’s recipe collection (Marcus + Claire).

## Plugins
- **Recipe View** (installed) — open a recipe note, then use the whisk / “Toggle recipe view” for a cook-friendly card (scales quantities, checkable ingredients). Works on phone.

## How we write recipes
One note per recipe under this folder. Prefer Instant Pot / air fryer / grill / one-pan, ≤20–30 min hands-on.

Suggested layout (plays nice with Recipe View):

```markdown
## Ingredients
- 2 lb ground beef
- 2 cans kidney beans
- 1 can pinto beans

## Method
1. Brown the beef.
2. Dump everything in the Instant Pot.
3. Pressure cook…

## Notes
Optional tips, leftovers, swaps.
```

- Put quantities at the **start** of ingredient bullets so scaling works
- Use a numbered list under Method/Steps
- Optional: thumbnail image at the top
