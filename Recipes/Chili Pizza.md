# Chili Pizza

Spice up a plain cheese pizza with leftover chili.

## Ingredients
- 1 cheese pizza (free / leftover)
- 1–1.5 cups leftover chili, slightly drained if very wet
- 0.5–1 cup shredded cheese
- Optional: salsa, diced onion, Greek yogurt after baking

## Method
1. Heat oven or air fryer to ~375°F.
2. Spoon chili over the pizza; scatter extra cheese on top.
3. Bake or air-fry until cheese melts and pizza is hot (about 5–10 min — watch so crust doesn’t burn).
4. Optional: dollop Greek yogurt or drizzle salsa after.

## Notes
- Better than microwave so the crust stays crisp.
- Good use for the “boring” free cheese pizza.
