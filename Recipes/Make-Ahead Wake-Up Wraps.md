# Make-Ahead Wake-Up Wraps

Dunkin-style breakfast wraps: scramble once on prep day, wrap, fridge/freeze, reheat in the morning. No morning scramble.

## Ingredients
- 8 flour tortillas (soft taco size)
- 8–10 eggs
- 0.5–0.75 lb smoked or Italian sausage, cooked and crumbled/sliced
- 1.5–2 cups shredded cheese
- 1 tbsp oil or butter for the pan
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: salsa for dipping after reheat

## Method
1. Cook sausage in a pan; set aside. Wipe pan if needed.
2. Scramble eggs in butter/oil until just set (slightly soft — they finish when reheating). Season.
3. Warm tortillas briefly so they don’t crack.
4. Fill each tortilla with egg, sausage, and cheese. Roll tight; tuck ends if you like burrito-style.
5. Wrap individually in foil or parchment. Fridge up to 4 days, or freeze.
6. **Morning:** air fry ~350°F for 5–8 min (from fridge) or oven 350°F ~10 min, until hot. From frozen, add a few minutes. Microwave works in a pinch (softer wrap).

## Notes
- Batch on Sun evening or a low-stress night.
- Pair with Greek yogurt or a banana if you want more staying power.
- Need tortillas if not already in the pantry.
